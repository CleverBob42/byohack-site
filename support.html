BYOHack Support

BYOHack helps you log meals, sync Apple Health activity, and get practical wellness coaching.

Common questions

A.I. limits: Free installs include meal logging, Health tracking, and 10 lifetime A.I. interactions. BYOHack Pro includes 15 A.I. interactions per calendar day (meal capture and Health Coach). Favourites re-log and manual edits do not use A.I. quota.

Subscriptions: Manage or cancel BYOHack Pro in iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Health data: Grant or revoke access in iOS Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → BYOHack.
